# reader-side paper audit

## 1. reader conclusion
- scope status: in scope
- evidence viability: non-auditable
- viability flags: self-referential-construct + circular-validation + critical-result-omission
- paper type: conceptual technical framework

The article introduces a new construct called Resonant Intelligence and defines it by a proprietary Resonance Score. It then treats a high Resonance Score as evidence that Resonant Intelligence exists and uses the same score to validate the framework. No independent behavioral, predictive, physical, or externally anchored outcome is supplied. The argument therefore closes on its own definitions rather than exposing a testable evidence chain.
